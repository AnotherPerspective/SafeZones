# Changelog

English first, Italian below. · Prima l'inglese, sotto l'italiano.

## 4.2 — 12 November 2026

- SafeZones is now open source, under the MIT License, and published on GitHub.
- Notices (AVVERTENZE.md) and all install guides in English and Italian.

## 4.2 — 5 October 2026

- Three versions in one package: DCTL plugin for DaVinci Resolve Studio, Fusion title (.drfx) for the DaVinci Resolve Edit page and Motion Graphics Template (.mogrt) for Adobe Premiere Pro.
- New interface graphics, sharp even in 4K, identical across all three versions.
- Measurements and controls unchanged from 4.0.
