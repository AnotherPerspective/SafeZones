## In full

**What it is.** While you edit, SafeZones draws guides over the image. They show the areas that the TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, LinkedIn, Snapchat and Pinterest apps cover on a vertical video, and can also show a mockup of the app. The package has three versions: an effect for DaVinci Resolve® Studio (a DCTL, a small program that Resolve runs on the image), a title for the Edit page of DaVinci Resolve (a Fusion title, .drfx) and a template for Adobe® Premiere Pro® (a Motion Graphics Template, .mogrt). It reads only the image it is applied to, inside the program. It does not change or delete files. It does not connect to the internet and sends no data to anyone.

**Free, experimental, open source.** SafeZones is free and still in development: it may contain errors, change, or stop working after an update of DaVinci Resolve or Premiere Pro. It is open source, under the MIT License: the text is in the [LICENSE.txt](LICENSE.txt) file and the source is on [GitHub](https://github.com/AnotherPerspective/SafeZones). You may use, copy, change and share it, also for commercial work. These notices take nothing away from the MIT License: they add to it.

**The measurements.** The safe zones come from screenshots we took on one phone (iPhone 13 Pro) in September 2026, and from the margins some platforms publish for ads. They are not official specifications. We do not promise that they match what a platform, a client or a broadcaster asks for. Apps change their interface often, and the space differs from phone to phone. Before you deliver, check the current specs of the platform.

**As is.** You get it as is, without any warranty: no promise that it works, that the sizes are right for your case or that it is free of errors.

**At your own risk.** Using it is your choice, and the consequences are yours: deliveries with the guides still visible, titles or text outside the safe zone, rejected material, working hours.

SafeZones is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of them.

**For professionals.** SafeZones is meant for video professionals who are used to checking their work before delivering it.

**With care.** Turn the guides off before exporting: in Resolve disable the adjustment clip or the track with the title, in Premiere turn off the track with the template. Watch the exported file before you deliver it. Check the specs of the platform you publish on.

**Future versions.** These notices apply to this version and to the next ones, "beta" or not, until we publish new ones.
